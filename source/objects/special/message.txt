Version Beta 4
-Parts
	If you shift-click the add object button you can now add "parts". Basically hands,feet,shirt,pants and arm. Gives you a little more control over character creation.
-Middle-Clicking
	If you middle-click the program will cycle through the move,scale and rotate tool.
-Object Loader
	Double-clicking and item on the list will load it into the scene.
